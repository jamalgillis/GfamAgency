# Sankofa SEO Good-Better-Best Pricing

This document captures the current Sankofa SEO and AI Search product ladder in a clear Good-Better-Best structure.

The intent behind this framing is psychological as much as operational:

- `Good` establishes a credible entry point.
- `Better` is the default value tier most qualified prospects should gravitate toward.
- `Best` positions total market control for brands with aggressive growth goals.

These offers are also reflected in the live service catalog as:

- `seo-local-entity-foundation`
- `seo-regional-authority-ai-engine`
- `seo-market-dominance-framework`

## Outcome Matrix

### Good: Local Entity Foundation

- Monthly investment: `$495 / mo`
- Best for: Single-location service providers, medical clinics, and local shops seeking fast local lead wins
- Goal: Lock down the immediate local perimeter, establish a Google Entity baseline, and capture active Waco-area searchers
- Core business outcome: Stronger local map visibility, near-me search performance, and phone call volume

The Entity & AI Engine:

- Complete Google Business Profile entity optimization
- Hyper-local citation building across trusted directory and business listing sources
- Review acquisition framework to strengthen sentiment and trust signals
- Essential on-page optimization for primary high-intent landing pages

### Better: Regional Authority & AI Engine

- Monthly investment: `$1,495 / mo`
- Best for: Growing mid-sized businesses, B2B firms, and service brands expanding across Central Texas
- Goal: Move beyond maps, capture non-branded search demand, and format the site to be read and cited by AI engines
- Core business outcome: Regional rankings growth, lower cost-per-lead, and more durable organic pipeline velocity

The Entity & AI Engine:

- Topical authority content clusters built around connected entities and related concepts
- Technical and semantic SEO improvements to support crawling, indexing, and machine understanding
- Regional landing page expansion for markets like Waco, Temple, Belton, and Killeen
- Core schema deployment, especially `Organization` and `LocalBusiness`

### Best: Market Dominance Framework

- Monthly investment: `$2,950 / mo`
- Best for: Multi-location brands, highly competitive niches, and businesses pushing aggressive revenue growth
- Goal: Capture digital market share, improve AI Overview visibility, and build enough authority to outmuscle larger out-of-market competitors
- Core business outcome: Broader market ownership, stronger organic revenue attribution, and long-term brand equity

The Entity & AI Engine:

- Advanced entity-linking and digital PR
- Comprehensive schema suite, including Product, FAQ, Service, and Video schema where applicable
- Conversion rate optimization to turn search growth into customers
- Deep content velocity and programmatic SEO support where the business model fits

## Positioning Notes

- `Good` gets the client on the board and stops the most immediate leakage to nearby competitors.
- `Better` is the anchor offer and should usually be the primary recommendation for qualified growth-minded prospects.
- `Best` is the strategic escalation path for brands that want category ownership, not just incremental lead flow.

## Suggested Pitch Track

- `Good`: "This gets you visible, credible, and competitive in your immediate local market."
- `Better`: "This is where SEO becomes a real asset instead of a maintenance task."
- `Best`: "This is for brands that want to own search territory, not just participate in it."

## Internal Notes

- Keep the language centered on outcomes first and tactics second.
- Lead with `Better` in proposal conversations unless the prospect is clearly price-constrained or clearly enterprise-scale.
- When discussing AI search, tie the concept back to concrete business visibility instead of abstract SEO jargon.
